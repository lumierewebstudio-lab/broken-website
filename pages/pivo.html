Featured Project

PIVO CONNECT. CREATE. DISCOVER.

A social marketplace designed around people, creativity and the work they want the world to discover.

The Concept

More than a marketplace.

PIVO brings social discovery and marketplace functionality together in one experience. People can showcase what they create, discover other work, connect with others and start conversations.

The Experience

Built around connection.

01

Showcase

Users can create posts to showcase their products, services, projects and creative work.

02

Discover

A social feed gives people a way to explore new work and discover creators, businesses and ideas.

03

Connect

Following and direct messaging create a more personal way for people to connect.

04

Share

Photos and videos help people communicate their work through visual storytelling.

The internet should make it easier to connect.
